Group review guide — development v1

The target is outside-investor events with specific issuer-directed actions. Activist hedge funds are a subgroup requiring evidence of both fund type and event intent. No performance or investment-quality claim follows from membership.

Review workflow

  1. Filter by manager/fund language and action language to locate candidates; these are unvalidated phrase hits.
  2. Open the full filing, read Item 2, Item 4, relevant Items 3/6 and exhibits. A phrase can be boilerplate or refer to a third party.
  3. Record final values and evidence in a dated copy of review-worksheet.csv. CIKs/CUSIPs are text identifiers; import them as text in Excel.
  4. Use document ID and zero-based Unicode [start,end) offsets from the bundle, plus exact quote. Preserve alternatives and unresolved references.
  5. Record reviewer and date. Freeze classifications before viewing outcomes. Keep manager verification separate from event-level intent.

Selection: 200 initial-index 13D candidates, 50 from each of 2013–15, 2016–18, 2019–21 and 2022–24; within each period target 30 manager-name-search, 10 action-text-search and 10 broad comparisons. Deterministic hash ranking, exact issuer/filer CIK-set deduplication, no outcome selection. Shortfalls can use broad candidates. Source-header amendments are rejected, so prepared count may be below 200. This purposive sample does not estimate population prevalence or validate out-of-sample returns. The original 32-filing development pack and ten exposed filings are excluded.

SEC source for reporting-person codes and Item 2: EDGAR Filer Manual, version 72. This guide is a new development aid; existing protocols remain unchanged.
