Machine Learning opening

I want the class project to center on a small neural network for post-13D investment selection. I now have 667 candidate events with filing and company inputs, plus chronological preprocessing. For the 2023 fold, 365 earlier outcomes are available for training and 86 later events for development evaluation. I plan to compare the network with simpler predictors, then ask whether validated LLM-extracted ownership and intent add information. No predictive model has been fitted yet.

Lead with the model comparison, not the HFR manager-ranking extension. Be ready to explain X versus y, training-only preprocessing, label maturity and why a negative result is useful.

  3. What model-capacity, tuning and extraction-validation evidence is expected for the course?
  4. Is honest chronological development reporting acceptable if no untouched historical evaluation is defensible? Confirm the submission dates and format.

Keep these distinctions clear

  • 667 events are not 667 managers; 556 have other/unresolved identities.
  • The ML target is a proposed return estimate. Whether, when, holding period and allocation are investment decisions built around forecasts and constraints.
  • Ownership imputation is a computational placeholder. Parser/citation checks do not certify semantic accuracy.
  • Repeatable deterministic extraction can still be wrong. LLM stability and source accuracy require separate checks.
  • Historical data have been examined; the later folds are development evidence. No alpha or fresh final test is claimed.
