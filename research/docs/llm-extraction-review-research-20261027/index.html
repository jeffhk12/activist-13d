Can LLMs review our 13D extractions?

October 27, 2026 · Literature probe complete · Reviewer experiment not run

Yes, it is worth testing an evidence-based automated audit. The useful design checks individual claims against filing text, separately searches for omitted information and tests whether reviewers catch known mistakes. Agreement alone does not provide an answer key. Jeff does not need to begin reviewing every extraction to investigate this approach.

Three different questions

QuestionProposed checkMeaning of the result
Is this extracted claim supported?A field critic sees the source, definition and candidate claim, with generator identity and rationale hiddenAn evidence-linked model judgment, with unresolved cases permitted
What did the extraction miss?A separate source-only pass searches before seeing the candidate answersCandidate missing people, ownership rows or objectives; still subject to shared omissions
Does the reviewer catch known mistakes?Clear controls with swapped actors, altered numbers, omitted rows or changed conditionsDetection on those controls, not the natural error rate of the filing dataset

Why the research matters here

Our saved intent labels agree in89/90 cells, yet funding types differ on three cases and ownership-row counts on one. A quotation can match the source exactly while the interpretation is too strong. For example, language allowing possible margin borrowing does not establish actual debt financing of that purchase.

The research brief distinguishes source support, omitted records, empirical agreement and reference-based accuracy. It includes peer-reviewed work and labelled preprints, with newer2026 studies and full-text section pointers. A new Federal Reserve working paper offers a conditional reconstruction-based route without human benchmark labels; it needs its own prerequisites and is a separate candidate to investigate.

What we can decide next

My preference is a bounded reviewer pilot using the existing discovery cases and known-fault controls. It would preserve the original extractions and treat proposed corrections as separate outputs. Model choice, control construction, review roles and reporting rules should be specified before running. No new filing batch or reserved-case review is underway.

If no independent semantic reference is available, report an automated audit with source-backed issues, abstentions and control performance. Natural-data precision, recall and false-pass rates remain unestimated. This is a way to investigate useful automation without giving Jeff a new labeling assignment.
