The timing issue, with a real example

Historical inputs must use information available before the investment decision. Giving the model results published later lets it learn from information an investor could not yet know; this is look-ahead bias.

The workbook selects the cutoff calendar year minus one as its annual fiscal period. In Live Fundamentals!A120:Q120, BHE has a January 3, 2019 cutoff and FY2018 values, including $2.566465 billion revenue and $58.538 million operating income. The company's FY2018 results release is dated February 7, 2019 and reports the same $58.538 million operating income. We cannot assume these annual results were known on January 3. This source check demonstrates a specific availability conflict; it does not measure how many of the 667 events are affected.

The controls themselves mark revision vintage as NOT VERIFIED and historical mapping as REVIEW REQUIRED. The file contains saved financial values and internal formulas, with no saved BQL retrieval formulas demonstrating an as-of query. Publication dates, vendor availability dates and revision-vintage evidence are absent. A historical fiscal year alone cannot rule out later restatements.

What differs from the collection plan

  • Comparable prior-year revenue, operating cash flow and company market capitalization are absent. Sales growth, cash-flow profitability and book-to-market therefore remain incomplete.
  • Revenue and income use annual periods; actual trailing-twelve-month periods were requested. Annual and TTM measures must remain distinguished.
  • Liabilities, operating income and EBITDA were added. Operating income is not operating cash flow.
  • Total debt is derived from assets and a debt/assets ratio, using divisor 100. The arithmetic agrees; the vendor definition and ratio units have not been independently validated.
  • All reviewed Bloomberg ID strings equal the original ticker seeds. This does not prove an error, but the workbook does not document independent historical-security validation.

Exceptions to review, without automatic correction

CDZI FY2017 has debt/assets of 208.18%; KMPH FY2020 has 629.38%. Ratios above 100% can be economically possible, so these flags do not establish a unit error or justify rescaling. PLUG FY2020 has revenue of −$93.237 million and needs source/definition review. Negative common equity or earnings are not automatically data errors. USD appears in output labels, but returned currency/scale metadata are not supplied for independent confirmation.

Next collection decision

  1. Use the existing pilot, including this early-January BHE case, to verify historical security identity, publication availability and revision vintage. Retain exact queries, returned metadata, units and sources. Select only statements available by each event cutoff.
  2. Collect the missing core fields and distinguish annual from actual TTM measures. Record unsupported fields explicitly rather than silently substituting metrics.
  3. Review unresolved coverage and missingness before defining a separately versioned feature extension. Preserve the frozen baseline and use the same chronological comparisons when evaluating added financial inputs.

The original workbook has not been modified, recalculated or refreshed. No neural network or other model was trained, and no return targets, cohort rules or frozen protocols were changed.
