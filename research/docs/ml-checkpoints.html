The methods, from the beginning

A baseline gives a simple prediction to beat. The training-mean baseline ignores the inputs and gives every later filing the average known training outcome. Training learns a rule from examples with known answers. A loss measures its mistakes; squared-error loss averages the squared differences between predictions and actual targets.

Ridge regression predicts an intercept plus a weighted combination of the prepared inputs, with a penalty on large weights. This penalty is regularization: it limits how much the model can adapt to historical patterns that may not repeat. We fixed the penalty at 1 under a mean-squared-error convention, without tuning it on these later years.

Example with invented numbers: two targets are -10 and +10 percentage points, and a prepared input is -1 and +1. Predicting zero for both gives RMSE of 10 points. Ridge with our declared penalty learns predictions of -5 and +5 points: it accepts some training error to keep its weight smaller. That improves this toy training fit; later examples are still needed to judge usefulness. The full lesson shows the arithmetic and actual training/later comparisons.

What this result establishes

This particular fixed linear model has not demonstrated useful prediction improvement. It does not settle whether another penalty, a neural network or added filing information could help. The model's later-year ranking correlations were also negative in all three years. These outcomes remain visible; no observations were trimmed and no parameters were changed after inspecting them.

The broad sample remains: 255 of the 302 evaluated events are other/unresolved investor candidates, alongside 18 focused activist, four other Event-Driven and 25 other HFR candidates. HFR labels are diagnostics, not predictors. Some training outcomes are very large; the largest stored target is about +2,960 percentage points and reconciles to its frozen stock-minus-benchmark calculation. Squared-error fitting is sensitive to large outcomes. This is a diagnostic, not proof of why the model failed.

Catch up before the neural network

Why can the training fit improve while later prediction gets worse?

The weights adapt to the training examples. Some relationships may be noise, may be dominated by unusual outcomes or may change over time. Later-year evaluation asks whether the learned relationships transfer. Better training fit alone does not establish that transfer.

Does an RMSE of 93.68 points mean a 93.68% return?

No. It summarizes the size of prediction errors on two-year benchmark-relative returns, with large misses receiving more weight. It is not a portfolio return, a hit rate or a probability.

The methods in plain language

MethodWhat it does13D example
Log transformCompresses the spread of large positive numbers while retaining their orderMarket capitalization and filing counts enter as log(1+x), reducing large numerical gaps
Median imputationFills a missing input with the middle value among observed training inputsA missing percentage uses the earlier training median, plus a flag showing it was missing
StandardizationExpresses a numerical value relative to the training mean and standard deviationSize and ownership can enter on comparable numerical scales despite different original units
One-hot encodingTurns a category into separate binary columnsIndustry 30 and industry 60 get separate columns; the code numbers do not imply magnitude
Chronological foldPairs earlier available training examples with a later prediction period2023 inputs use only label windows finished before 2023; future rows cannot reset the scaling

Some columns never vary in a training fold, so they are removed using that training fold alone. Original missingness stays in the source preview and diagnostics. Later unseen industries are encoded as all-zero category vectors and listed as unknown, rather than added to the vocabulary using future data.

What remains uncertain

These are mechanical gap categories, not semantic review of the filings. Filling these inputs does not make the extraction correct.

Market data are dated historical vendor observations; vendor publication timing is not certified. Confirmed HFR identity features, liquidity, volatility and fundamentals remain outside this preview. Catch up before the next method

Why does an 8.65% fill value not mean the missing stake was probably 8.65%?

It is a declared computational replacement based on the training median. It allows the model to process the row. The missing-value flag and original source record preserve the uncertainty.

Why are there 69 inputs if we started with ten fields?

Industry was expanded into 58 binary category columns in the 2023 fold. Numerical inputs and missing flags supplied the remaining retained columns. Columns with no training variation were removed.

Why not let the 2023 examples help choose the scaling?

The simulated 2023 prediction must use settings established from earlier available examples. Future inputs changing those settings would leak future information into the procedure.
