The methods in plain language

MethodWhat it does13D example
Log transformCompresses the spread of large positive numbers while retaining their orderMarket capitalization and filing counts enter as log(1+x), reducing large numerical gaps
Median imputationFills a missing input with the middle value among observed training inputsA missing percentage uses the earlier training median, plus a flag showing it was missing
StandardizationExpresses a numerical value relative to the training mean and standard deviationSize and ownership can enter on comparable numerical scales despite different original units
One-hot encodingTurns a category into separate binary columnsIndustry 30 and industry 60 get separate columns; the code numbers do not imply magnitude
Chronological foldPairs earlier available training examples with a later prediction period2023 inputs use only label windows finished before 2023; future rows cannot reset the scaling

Some columns never vary in a training fold, so they are removed using that training fold alone. Original missingness stays in the source preview and diagnostics. Later unseen industries are encoded as all-zero category vectors and listed as unknown, rather than added to the vocabulary using future data.

What the checks establish

Synthetic tests pin median filling, population-standard-deviation scaling, log transforms, unknown category handling and cutoff boundaries. A test verifies that transforming later extreme inputs leaves fitted settings unchanged. Additional checks verify row alignment, source-preview hashes, finite input tables and exact offline replay. This is local validation; no remote CI result is claimed.

What remains uncertain

Ownership summaries are missing in 110 of 667 events: 89 have no present percentage in their extracted ownership rows, seven have no extracted ownership rows for the matched security, and 14 have unresolved ownership/security attribution. These are mechanical gap categories, not semantic review of the filings. Filling these inputs does not make the extraction correct.

Market data are dated historical vendor observations; vendor publication timing is not certified. Confirmed HFR identity features, liquidity, volatility and fundamentals remain outside this preview. No predictive model has been trained, no accuracy has been measured and no investment edge is established.

Catch up before the next method

Why does an 8.65% fill value not mean the missing stake was probably 8.65%?

It is a declared computational replacement based on the training median. It allows the model to process the row. The missing-value flag and original source record preserve the uncertainty.

Why are there 69 inputs if we started with ten fields?

Industry was expanded into 58 binary category columns in the 2023 fold. Numerical inputs and missing flags supplied the remaining retained columns. Columns with no training variation were removed.

Why not let the 2023 examples help choose the scaling?

The simulated 2023 prediction must use settings established from earlier available examples. Future inputs changing those settings would leak future information into the procedure.

Next checkpoint: define the first predictive model experiment and its loss, comparison predictions and evaluation. The input dimension and limited completed history will guide a small neural network and a modest tuning budget. Neural-network success is a question to test, not a premise.
